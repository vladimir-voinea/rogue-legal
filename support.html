Rogue — Support

Rogue is a private, offline AI assistant for iPhone and iPad. If something is not working, write to parallax.tech.ro@gmail.com — please say which device and iOS version you are on, and what you asked Rogue to do.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
Not to talk to the model. You need one once, to download the model you want to use.
Where are my conversations stored?
On your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.
Rogue says a tool cannot run on this device. Why?
Some tools need hardware or a newer iOS version — a camera, a scanner, haptics, or iOS 26 for certain alarm features. Rogue tells you instead of pretending it worked.
Why does an alarm set by Rogue not appear in the Clock app?
Rogue's alarms belong to Rogue. They ring through silent mode and Focus, and you manage them in Rogue — they are not listed in Apple's Clock app, which only lists its own alarms.
How do I turn a tool off?
Open the Tools screen and switch it off. Every tool is off-limits to the model until you allow it.
Which models can I use?
Any model offered in the Models screen. Smaller models answer faster; larger ones are more capable. You can switch models per conversation.

Reporting a bug

Email the address above with the steps you took, what you expected, and what happened. If a run misbehaved, the Ledger in the conversation shows each step Rogue took — including it in your message helps a great deal.
